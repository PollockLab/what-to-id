How the test works

  1. The question. Does the order of records change how many get an ID, and which?
  2. The lists. Each record goes to one list at random, so every list holds the same mix of species and observers. Only the order is different:
    • Newest first. The control, close to what iNaturalist shows today.
    • Data-poor places first. Records from areas with few or old records.
  3. Your batches. Each press of Next batch takes the next list in turn. Your browser picks the turn order at random. The page does not say which list a batch is from.
  4. The count. After the blitz, we count each participant's species-level IDs on each list and compare each person with themself. A fast identifier adds the same to every list.
  5. Nothing else changes. You identify in iNaturalist as usual. Your IDs carry your name, count toward Research Grade and go to GBIF.
