Confused pairs

A photo is confused when, to the model, it matches another species' name better than its own label. Pick a group, then a pair: its records come worst first, the ones that look most like the other species.

How this list is made
Model
BioCLIP 2.5 (ViT-H/14). Each photo gets a match score (cosine) against the text "a photo of <species>" for every species name. Its margin is the score of its own label minus the best score of any other species. The reference is text only. The proposals are never the reference.
Confused
The margin is below 0, at any rank.
Listed
A species needs at least 4 scored photos. A pair needs at least 2 of its photos pointing to the same other species. Each species lists at most 3 pairs.
Share
Confused photos divided by that species' scored photos.
Records
Up to 50 records per pair, the most negative margin first.
