    How this is made

    A card’s rank is the pair’s p in the metaweb times the evidence: the field names the pair, the field names a coarse ancestor of one animal, the description, tags or field text mention a candidate prey, or they only talk about food. Cards link out to iNaturalist; add the prey as the Eating field there if the photo shows it.

    Class filter: limits the cards to predators of one class (birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians or insects), read from the ancestors in the metaweb. A predator whose class the metaweb does not know is left out while a class is chosen.

    Three checks per card: is this the predator, is this the prey, and is the predator eating the prey, each answered yes, no or can’t tell. A card counts as shows it, in the confirmed network and the exports, only when the third is yes and neither of the first two is no. Marks saved before the three checks load as the same answer to all three.

    Export network: a CSV for an ecology pipeline, with # key: value header lines (question, metaweb, date, shown, the photos shown; read it with comment="#"), then one row per predator-prey pair of the question. Columns: predator, predator_taxon_id, prey, prey_taxon_id (blank when no observation names the taxon), status (confirmed_here first photograph, photographed_before, nothing_found searched without a confirmed mark, not_searched), shows_it, not_visible, cant_tell (marks for the pair), shows_it_observation_ids (separated by ;), prior_records (observations whose field names the pair), metaweb_p. Export annotations: one row per mark, observation_id, url, predator, prey, answer, date, then is_predator, is_prey, is_eating (the three checks).
