Identify

Records sorted for review by how each photo matches its label.

About

Each record's first photo is compared with the names of the species in the pool by BioCLIP 2.5 (ViT-H/14), an image and text model trained on the tree of life. A match score is the cosine between the photo's embedding and the embedding of the text "a photo of <species>". The margin is the match score of the label minus the best match score of any other species. __SCORING_DATES__

A record takes the first level whose rule it meets, in this order:

__LEVEL_1__
Labelled at species. The margin is above 0, and the record is in the top 25% of its species by match score to the label. Only species with 4 or more scored records.
__LEVEL_3__
Labelled above species (a genus or a family, say), and the pool names one or more species inside the label. Every such record goes here, whatever its match scores.
__LEVEL_5__
Three kinds of record, in this order: might be mislabelled (lowest margin first), odd shots, close calls. This order put the records iNaturalist identifiers later corrected nearer the top than mixing the three did. Odd shots: any rank, the photo's best match score to any species is in the lowest 2% of its group (birds, plants, fungi and so on). Close calls: labelled at species, one other species scores higher than the label by 0.02 or less and has 20 or more verifiable iNaturalist records in BC. Might be mislabelled: labelled at species, the margin is below −0.02 and the best other species has 20 or more verifiable iNaturalist records in BC. Close calls and might-be-mislabelled cards name that species and show a reference photo of it beside one of the label, from iNaturalist.
No level
All other records: the margin is above 0 but the record is outside the top 25% of its species, or its species has fewer than 4 records; the look-alike has fewer than 20 BC records; the pool names no species inside the label, or the label is a species that was not in the pool at scoring; the record was not in the __PHOTO_DOWNLOAD__ (most were added after it); or its label changed after scoring.

A level sorts records for review. It does not say that a label is right or wrong. How it works: Methods gives the model, every cut-off, and a test of the levels against later corrections.

The User box is the map's: with an iNaturalist login it lists first the records in the user's genera, those holding about 80% of their identifications in BC (Adjust sets a number of IDs per genus instead), then a line, then the rest; the map dims the rest. Ticks narrow it: Only my genera, Hide records I identified, or Only records I observed. The login is kept on this device for a day. The login goes only to iNaturalist.
